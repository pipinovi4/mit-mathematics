## Find the tangent line to $y = f(x)$ at $P = (x_0,y_0)$

### Tangent line equation 

> $y - y_0 = m(x - x_0)$  
> point $y_0 = f(x_0)$  
> slope $m = f'(x_0)$ 

### Definition 

> $f'(x_0)$ the derivative of at $x_0$, is the slope of the tangent line to $y = f(x)$ at P.
